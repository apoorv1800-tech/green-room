The problem

Most pitches fail in the first 20 seconds, and nobody tells you why.

One pitch, every room

Candidates use the same script for a bank MD and a startup founder. What impresses one bores the other.

Mock interviews are scarce

Seniors and career coaches have limited slots, usually right before placement season when you need them most.

Feedback is vague

"Make it crisper" doesn't help. You need to know which line to cut and what to say instead.

How it works

2

3

Get the room's reaction

A score, your three weakest lines rewritten, and the follow-up question they'll most likely ask.
